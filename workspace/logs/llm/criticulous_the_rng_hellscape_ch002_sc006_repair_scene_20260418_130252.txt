"Did you catch the math on that?" Artie asked, adjusting the oversized Cloak of the Cowardly Lion on his shoulders so it wouldn't snag his throwing arm.

Artie grinned. His 1/1 HP was a terrifying liability, but right now? It was the key to the kingdom. He checked that his Rusty Iron Dagger was securely stowed at his belt. He wouldn't need the blade. He had rocks. He wound up and lobbed a jagged piece of crypt masonry at a skeleton limping toward him from the shadows. The rock pinged off the monster's skull with a pathetic clack.
