Previously, I was planning the narrative and mechanics for "Scene 2" of a story or game involving a character named Artie. 

Specifically, I was focusing on the sequence where Artie reaches into a vending machine and gets his hand stuck near the internal wiring. While planning this, I was keeping a strict world-building constraint in mind: no User Interface (UI) elements are allowed to be shown or described yet, because Artie is still in the "real world." This implies that getting shocked or stuck in the machine will likely trigger his transition into a digital or game world where UI elements *will* eventually appear.
