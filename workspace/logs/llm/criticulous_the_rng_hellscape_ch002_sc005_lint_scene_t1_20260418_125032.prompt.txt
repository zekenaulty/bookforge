Artie stared down the skeletal guard he had just aggroed, his heart hammering against his ribs in a frantic, terrifying rhythm. He scrambled backward across the damp stone of the crypt, his boots slipping on patches of slick, luminescent moss. The Cloak of the Cowardly Lion whimpering around his shoulders didn't help his nerves; the oversized garment trembled like a frightened dog, its magical stealth completely ruined by its own audible anxiety. 

The crypt itself was a masterclass in generic fantasy misery. Water dripped from the arched ceiling, pooling in uneven divots on the floor. The air smelled of ozone, old dust, and the distinct, metallic tang of dried blood. 

[Target: Skeletal Guard (Lvl. 8) – HP: 120/120]

The skeleton didn't care about the whimpering cloak. It raised a chipped, rusty iron sword, its red eyes burning with digital malice inside an empty skull. Every step it took produced a sickening clack-clack-clack of bone against stone. 

Artie gripped the Rusty Iron Dagger in his right hand so tightly his knuckles turned white. The weapon felt entirely useless. To use it, he'd have to get into melee range, and with 1 HP, a single blocked strike would kill him. 

The skeleton lunged, its bony feet clattering loudly as it closed the distance with terrifying speed. It swung the sword in a wide, horizontal arc aimed perfectly at Artie's midsection.

Artie threw himself backward, dodging the blade by a terrifyingly narrow margin. He dropped to one knee, his left hand blindly searching the debris-littered floor. His fingers brushed against broken glass, wet moss, and finally closed around a jagged, loose rock. 

He didn't think about his stance. He just cocked his arm back and hurled the stone with all the desperate, uncoordinated strength of a panicked accountant trying to survive a Monday morning audit. 

The rock sailed through the dank air and clocked the skeletal guard right between its glowing red eyes with a hollow, resonant thwack.

[Critical Hit! -99,999 HP]

The massive, glowing red numbers exploded into the air, illuminating the gloomy crypt like a distress flare. The skeletal guard froze mid-swing. Then, with a sound like a collapsing glass chandelier, the monster completely shattered into a fine mist of white dust and tiny, pulverized bone splinters. 

[Target Defeated.]

[Experience Gained: 150 XP]

Artie stayed on his knees, panting heavily. He slowly lowered the Rusty Iron Dagger, his chest heaving as the adrenaline began to ebb.

Fizz zipped down from the ceiling, her digital blue eyes wide and her neon pink hair flaring with static electricity. She hovered over the small pile of dust.

"Uh, Artie?" Fizz said, her voice tinged with systemic concern. "I think you broke its math."

"I threw a rock," Artie said, his voice trembling slightly as he stared at his empty left hand. "I didn't even throw it that hard."

"You have a one hundred percent critical hit rate," Fizz reminded him. "And your crit multiplier is currently returning a null value, which the system defaults to the maximum integer limit."

Before he could process the absurdity of that statement, the air above the remains began to violently glitch. A wireframe box materialized out of nowhere, rapidly cycling through item icons while emitting a high-pitched screech.

[System Error: Overkill threshold exceeded by 99,900%. Entity loot table rupture detected.]

[Warning: Physics engine stress test failed.]

[Dumping complete loot table...]

Artie scrambled backward again as a geyser of items erupted from the floor. It started with a fountain of gold coins, spraying outward like a busted fire hydrant and clattering loudly against the stone walls. But it didn't stop there. Glowing bone fragments, dozens of rusty swords, tattered leather scraps, minor health potions, and random pieces of low-level armor rained down in a chaotic, absurd cascade. 

"Take cover!" Fizz yelled, darting behind Artie's head. 

Artie threw his arms over his head, the Cloak of the Cowardly Lion whimpering loudly as it tried to wrap itself around his face for protection. The clatter of falling loot echoed through the crypt for a full ten seconds before finally tapering off.

When the dust finally settled, Artie slowly stood up. The crypt floor was completely unrecognizable. Where there had once been damp stone and a single pile of bone dust, there was now a mound of treasure that reached Artie's waist. 

He stepped closer, his boots crunching on a thick carpet of gold coins. He reached out and picked up a glowing blue bone fragment from the top of the pile. 

[Item: Flawless Femur of the Forgotten. Drop Rate: 0.01%.]

He dropped it back onto the pile. 

"Fizz," Artie muttered, his eyes wide as his accounting brain automatically began to calculate the gross value of the hoard. "Did it just drop everything?"

"Everything," Fizz confirmed, floating out from behind him. "Every item on its loot table. The overkill damage was so high that the system's loot generation formula experienced a buffer overflow. It couldn't calculate the probability, so it just gave you everything to prevent a fatal crash."

Artie stared at the absurd pile of loot left by the shattered skeleton. He wasn't just a glass cannon. He was a walking exploit. He could turn any low-level enemy into a localized economic crisis with a single tap. 

Artie chuckled, slipping the Rusty Iron Dagger into his belt. "Fizz, how many more skeletons are in this place?"

"According to the map? Hundreds," Fizz replied, narrowing her eyes. "Why?"

"Because I think I just found my new career path. We're going to need a bigger wallet."
