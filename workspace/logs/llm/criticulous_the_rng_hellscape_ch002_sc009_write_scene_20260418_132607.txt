PROSE:
Artie stood before the obsidian pedestal of the Boss Trigger, calculating the exponential XP payout in his head while Fizz darted back and forth across his vision, begging him to reconsider. The crystal resting atop the dark stone pulsed with an ominous, deep violet light, casting long, erratic shadows across the circular expanse of the lower crypt. 

"Artie, please, I am running the simulations and the math is completely catastrophic!" Fizz shrieked, her digital_blue eyes wide with genuine panic. She manifested a barrage of holographic windows, plastering them directly between Artie and the pedestal. 

`[System Warning: Extreme Danger. Recommended Level: 35+]`
`[Current Level: 3. Probability of Survival: 0.0001%]`

Artie reached up and swiped his hand through the glowing red error messages, dismissing them into bursts of pixelated dust. He adjusted the heavy, oversized folds of the Cloak of the Cowardly Lion around his shoulders. The stolen garment was ridiculously warm, but it did little to ward off the chill of the damp stone room. 

"Your math is flawed, Fizz," Artie said, his voice echoing slightly in the massive, empty chamber. He tapped the side of his head. "You're looking at this like a standard game progression. You're looking at the level gap. I'm looking at the core variables."

"The core variable is that it will crush you into a fine paste!" Fizz flew in a tight circle around his head, her glowing neon_pink hair trailing like a comet's tail. "This is a Floor Guardian! It has area-of-effect attacks, environmental hazards, and a health pool that requires a coordinated raid party of at least ten people to whittle down over forty-five minutes!"

Artie rested his hands on his hips, his thumb brushing against the hilt of the Rusty Iron Dagger stowed at his belt. "Let's review my character sheet, shall we? What is my maximum health?"

Fizz paused, hovering just above the violet crystal. She crossed her tiny, translucent arms. "One."

"Exactly. One HP." Artie pointed a finger at the AI. "If a level two skeletal rat bites my ankle, I die instantly. If a level thirty-five Floor Guardian drops the ceiling on my head, I die instantly. There is literally zero difference in the risk. My fragility is absolute. Therefore, since the risk is a flat plateau of immediate death, the only variable that actually matters is the reward."

"That is the most deranged application of risk assessment I have ever processed," Fizz whispered, her digital form glitching slightly with stress. 

"It's basic accounting," Artie countered, stepping closer to the pedestal. The cursed Participation Trophy pinned to his lapel caught the violet light, gleaming with a dull, mocking shine. "And let's not forget the other half of the equation. Vane. That arrogant, shiny-armored jerk came down here, cleared out all the annoying trash mobs, and left the big payout behind because he didn't want to share the XP with his guild, or maybe he just wanted to save it for a rainy day. He left his piggy bank right here in the open."

"It's not a piggy bank, it's a summoning crystal!" 

"It's unearned capital," Artie corrected. "And I have a one-hundred percent critical hit rate. I just need to hit it once, Fizz. One rock, one dagger thrust, one lucky tap. The system math is broken in my favor. If I kill this thing, I jump high enough in level to actually survive whatever Vane tries to do to me when he catches up."

Fizz manifested a massive, flashing stop sign. "Artie, bosses have mechanics! They have invulnerability frames! They have—"

Artie ignored her. He stepped up to the pedestal, raising his hand over the pulsing violet gem. The air in the room grew heavy, thick with the smell of ozone and ancient dust. 

"Time to audit the dungeon," Artie muttered. 

Artie slammed his hand on the crystal, sealing the doors as the Floor Guardian began to spawn. 

`[Boss Summoning Initiated]`

The violet light exploded outward, washing the entire chamber in a blinding flash. Behind them, the massive iron-reinforced stone doors slammed shut with a deafening crash. Heavy locking mechanisms ground into place, sealing them inside. 

`[Room Lock Engaged]`
`[Warning: Encounter Cannot Be Fled]`

The floor beneath their feet began to tremble as the obsidian pedestal sank into the ground, and a massive, swirling vortex of bone and dark magic began to coalesce in the center of the room.
