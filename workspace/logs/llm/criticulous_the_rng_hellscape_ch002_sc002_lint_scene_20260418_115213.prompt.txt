[Starter Quest: The Unbreakable Lock]

Artie stared at the massive steel padlock. "So, I need to find a rogue?"

"No," Fizz said, her digital eyes narrowing into glowing crescents. "You are a walking system error. You don't need lockpicking."

Artie pulled up his own status screen, the grim reality of his glitched build flashing before his eyes.

[HP: 1/1 | Stamina: 10/10 | Crit Rate: 100%]

He looked from his pathetic single hit point to the guaranteed critical strike. The System said the lock was unbreakable because it had a massive health pool and high defense. But a critical hit bypassed standard damage mitigation. If a simple kick could vaporize a slime and deal ninety-nine thousand damage, what would it do to a piece of tutorial hardware?

It was a terrible idea. If the chest had a damage reflection mechanic, his 1 HP would instantly vanish, sending him back to the void. But standing around in an oversized suit with a cursed golden pin wasn't going to keep him alive either.

He needed that cloak.

Artie glanced around the plaza, noticing a discarded Rusty Iron Dagger lying near a battered training dummy. He scooped it up, the coarse metal heavy in his right hand. It wasn't much, but it was better than breaking his knuckles.

Making his decision, Artie stepped past the painted warning sign. He gripped the hilt tightly, his eyes locked on the heavy iron padlock. Taking a deep breath, Artie prepared to strike the unbreakable chest.
