PROSE:
Artie reached the edge of the Tutorial Forest, the pixelated pine needles giving way to a worn dirt path, only to be intercepted by Vane. A shadow blotted out the digital sun as the massive figure stepped into the clearing. Vane was an overly designed fantasy warlord bristling with unnecessary armor spikes. A dark Guild Master Tattoo marked his left cheek, standing out starkly against his tanned skin and spiky silver hair.

"Well, well," Vane rumbled, his crimson eyes locking onto Artie's chest. "A golden-tier drop from a tutorial mob? You must have some insane RNG, noob."

Artie glanced down at his lapel. The cursed Participation Trophy gleamed with an obnoxious, unearned luster. "This? It's literally just a piece of plastic. It says 'You Tried' on the bottom."

"Don't play dumb," Vane sneered, crossing his thick, muscular arms. The massive Obsidian Greatsword strapped to his back scabbard shifted with the movement, the dark, glass-like blade humming with latent energy. "I've been farming this zone for three days. Hand it over, or I take it the hard way."

Before Artie could explain that the cursed item was permanently glued to his cheap suit, a red holographic window shattered the air between them.

[System Notification: Duel Request Received!]
[Challenger: Vane (Lvl. 15) – HP: 500/500]
[Condition: Deathmatch]
[Wager: Equipped Item (Participation Trophy)]

Artie stared at the floating red text, his heart hammering against his ribs. Level fifteen? Five hundred health? 

"Accept the duel," Vane demanded, his hand dropping to the hilt of his greatsword. "Or I flag up and player-kill you right here. Your choice."

Artie pulled up his own status bar.

[HP: 1/1]

Right. A single tap from that oversized butter knife, a stiff breeze, or even a particularly aggressive sneeze from the warlord would shatter Artie's single hit point into digital dust. His one-hundred-percent critical hit rate wouldn't matter at all if he was sliced in half before he could even throw a punch. 

Artie's brown eyes darted past Vane's spiked shoulder pads, scanning the fortified stone walls in the distance. The heavy iron gates of Oakhaven were just down the path, bustling with the noise of a passing merchant caravan. 

He swallowed hard. Artie realized he could not win a fair fight and looked for an escape.
