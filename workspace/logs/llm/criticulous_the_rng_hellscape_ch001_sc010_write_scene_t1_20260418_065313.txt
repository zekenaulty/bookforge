PROSE:
Staring at the glowing red prompt, Artie realizes he cannot win a fair fight and desperately looks for an escape. Vane stood blocking the dirt path, his polished silver armor gleaming and his massive obsidian greatsword resting casually on one shoulder. 

`[System Alert: Duel Request from Vane – Deathmatch]`

The prompt hovered in the air between them, the timer ticking down. Artie didn't need to check his stats, but his peripheral vision helpfully flashed his most terrifying metric anyway.

`[HP: 1/1]`

One hit. A glancing blow, a stiff breeze, or a particularly aggressive sneeze from the armored giant would send Artie straight back to the void. He patted his chest, feeling the heavy lump of his Leather Wallet stowed safely in his right pocket, the plastic edge of his Employee ID Badge dangling from his neck lanyard, and the cursed Participation Trophy firmly pinned to his lapel. The golden-tier junk item pulsed with a faint, mocking light, drawing Vane's greedy gaze.

"Just accept the duel, Anomaly," Vane sneered, taking a heavy, deliberate step forward. "I'll make it quick. I just want the drop."

Artie backed up, his cheap suit shoes slipping on the loose dirt. He brushed a hand through his messy brown hair, sweat beading on his pale skin. "I'd love to, really, but my schedule is packed. Lots of synergizing to do. Quarterly reviews."

Before Vane could swing, a cacophony of snorts, creaking wood, and shouting erupted from the main road. A massive merchant caravan—comprised of six oversized wagons pulled by six-legged, scaly beasts of burden—barreled toward the fortified iron gates of Oakhaven. Dust kicked up in thick, choking clouds.

"Make way!" a merchant screamed from the lead wagon, cracking a glowing whip. 

It was the only chance. Artie threw himself forward, not at Vane, but toward the side of the passing caravan. He squeezed between a massive, scaly flank and the spinning wooden wheels of the second wagon, instantly breaking Vane's line of sight.

"Hey! Get back here!" Vane roared, the heavy *thud* of his boots momentarily drowned out by the bellowing draft beasts.

Artie didn't look back. He kept his head down, using the massive wagons as a moving wall. He matched their frantic pace as they rolled over the cobblestone threshold, slipping right past the heavily armed city guards. The heavy iron portcullis loomed overhead, casting a brief shadow before Artie burst out into the bustling, sunlit expanse of the central square.

A soft blue chime rang in his ears, instantly shattering the blaring red of the duel challenge.

`[Area Discovered: Oakhaven City Proper]`
`[Safe Zone Active. PvP Combat Disabled.]`

Artie collapsed against a wooden fruit stall, gasping for breath. He checked his lapel. The Participation Trophy was still there, but more importantly, he was still breathing. He had made it into the city.
