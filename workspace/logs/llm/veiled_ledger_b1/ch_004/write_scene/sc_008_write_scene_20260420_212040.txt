PROSE:
Immediately following the terminal's core purge, the shrill alarms cut out, leaving the damp chamber bathed in a dull amber light. Rhea holds the pulsing Raw Data Packet in her left hand as the system attempts to violently reclaim it. A holographic meter hovers over the jagged crystal, its durability decaying by the second.

[System Warning: Unauthorized Asset Detected. Initiating Reclaim Purge.]

[Item Durability: 42%]

Vance groans, rolling onto his back on the stone floor. He cradles his blistered hands against his chest, his breath coming in ragged, wet gasps. "Tell me you got it," he wheezes, his HP bar hovering at a terrifyingly low three percent.

Nearby, Unit 734 twitches, plumes of thick smoke venting from its smoking chassis. "Efficiency at twelve percent," the drone stutters, its optical sensor flickering. "Local node sweep imminent. Asset deletion protocol engaged."

[Item Durability: 28%]

"I have it," Rhea says, her eyes locked on the rapidly decaying numbers. Her forearms sting where her singed arm hair rubs against her torn jacket, but she ignores the pain. The crystal burns cold against her palm. She pulls up her standard interface, attempting to drag the Raw Data Packet into her general inventory.

[Action Denied: Asset flagged for administrative deletion.]

[Item Durability: 19%]

The system was fast. It didn't care that they had bled for the proof; it only cared about the ledger. If it was an asset, the local overseer could seize it. But Rhea wasn't going to play by the rules of standard asset management. 

"Vance, Unit 734," Rhea snaps, her fingers flying across the glowing blue interface. "Acknowledge party contract override."

"What?" Vance coughs, wincing as he shifts his weight.

"Do it, or we die broke!" she barks, pulling up the scrolling green text of their custom agreement. 

[Party Contract: The Mercy Cache (Provisional) - Active]

She scrolls past the loot distribution tables, bypassing the friendly-fire restrictions, and slams her finger into the punitive clauses she had drafted back at the kiosk. Clause 4.B: Shared Debt and Liabilities. The system was rigged to punish debt, to bind it to the soul of the indentured so they could never escape it. Rhea was going to use that exact predatory logic.

[Item Durability: 8%]

She selects the Raw Data Packet and drags the icon directly into the party's shared debt ledger, reclassifying the stolen administrative logs not as a valuable quest item, but as a catastrophic legal penalty. 

[Designating 'Raw Data Packet' as Soul-Bound Liability...]

[Processing...]

The crystal in her hand begins to dissolve into blue ash. 

[Item Durability: 3%]

[Item Durability: 1%]

"Confirm!" Rhea shouts. 

"Confirmed!" Vance groans, tapping his bruised interface.

"Affirmative," Unit 734 whirs.

[System Override: Liability Accepted. Applying Mutual Assured Destruction parameters.]

[Inventory Update: Raw Data Packet (Soul-Bound Liability) - Locked.]

The decay halts instantly. The blue ash solidifies, the crystal dematerializing from her palm and locking into a permanent, unalterable slot within her personal system inventory. The pulsing light fades, leaving only the dim amber glow of the trenches. 

Rhea lets out a slow breath, closing the interface. The math was finally on their side. The data is secured as a liability, completely safe from the local admin's reach.
