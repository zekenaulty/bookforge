PROSE:
Immediately following the discovery of the routing destination, the brass terminal's automated defenses triggered a localized memory wipe.

A shrill, ear-splitting alarm tore through the damp air of the Maintenance Trenches. The massive server terminal, which moments ago had been leaking a serene blue light, suddenly flashed a violent, strobing crimson.

[System Warning: Unauthorized Access to Account #001-Alpha.]
[Automated Defenses Engaged.]
[Protocol: Localized Memory Wipe Initiated.]
[Memory Wipe Progress: 2%]

"What did you do?" Vance groaned, his voice barely audible over the klaxons. He was slumped against the cavern wall, clutching his side. The glowing red bar above his head was a terrifyingly thin sliver.

[HP: 3/100]

"I found the bottom of the rabbit hole," Rhea yelled back, her eyes darting across the terminal's shifting surface. "And the rabbit is trying to burn the evidence."

She looked down at the Scrap Parchment in her left hand, where she had hastily scribbled the offshore account number using the Charcoal Stick. It was proof, yes, but in the Ledger's bureaucratic nightmare of a legal system, a handwritten note from a Level 1 indentured arrival wouldn't hold up against a local Overseer. She needed the source code. She needed the raw data packet itself.

[Memory Wipe Progress: 8%]

The terminal groaned. Thick brass gears within its chassis began to grind, shifting heavy metal plates to seal off the glowing blue conduits. The machine was intentionally overheating itself, preparing to slag its own memory drives to protect the embezzlement.

"Unit 734!" Rhea barked. "Halt the wipe!"

The drone bobbed erratically, its single optical lens whirring as it tried to process the conflicting directives.

[Unit 734 Efficiency: 38%]

"Negative. Negative," the drone stuttered, its synthesized voice pitching upward in panic. "I am a low-level maintenance construct. I do not possess the administrative clearance to override a localized memory wipe. Attempting to do so will result in my immediate disassembly!"

"If that data burns, we have no leverage!" Rhea snapped. "And if we have no leverage, the Overseer is going to audit us until we're dust. Do something!"

[Memory Wipe Progress: 15%]

The temperature in the chamber was rising rapidly. The damp air turned stifling, smelling of old copper, burning ozone, and Vance's blood. The terminal's primary cooling vents—four heavy, louvered brass grates on the front of the chassis—began to slide shut, locking the heat inside to accelerate the meltdown.

Rhea didn't have time to argue. She shoved the Scrap Parchment and the Charcoal Stick into her right blazer pocket, freeing her left hand. A second later, she dropped her heavy Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon to the stone floor. It hit with a dull clatter, useless against software. She needed both hands.

She lunged at the terminal, grabbing the edge of the nearest cooling vent just before it sealed shut. The brass was already uncomfortably hot. She pulled, planting her boots against the base of the machine, throwing her entire body weight into the effort.

[Stamina: 18/100]

The metal didn't budge. The gears grinding inside the terminal were powered by the local node's infinite mana reserves. Rhea was powered by a fraction of a beginner's stamina pool and a half-digested glowing fungus.

"It's locking down!" Rhea grunted, her boots slipping on the damp stone floor. "If these vents close, the core melts. The data packet is in there!"

[Memory Wipe Progress: 24%]

"Let it burn," Vance rasped. He coughed, a wet, terrible sound that sent a spray of red mist into the air. "We need to run. The construct is dead. The alarm is loud. More will come."

"Run where, Vance?" Rhea shot back, her knuckles turning white as she strained against the vent. "To the surface? Where the Overseer controls the air we breathe and the taxes on our footsteps? If we don't get this proof, we are dead. It's just a matter of how many days it takes for our health bars to hit zero."

[Stamina: 15/100]

Her grip slipped. The vent slid another inch closed, the heat blasting out from the gap singeing the hairs on her arms. Deep inside the chassis, behind the closing grates, she could see it—a pulsing, crystalline structure suspended in a web of blue hard-light. The raw data packet.

"Vance!" Rhea screamed, panic finally bleeding into her voice. "I can't hold it! I don't have the stats!"

Vance closed his eyes. His scarred face contorted in agony. He was a man who had survived the tutorial by knowing exactly when to fight and when to hide. Grabbing a superheated brass vent while sitting at three hit points was not survival. It was suicide.

But the glowing green text of their custom party contract still hovered in the periphery of his vision. Mutual Assured Destruction. If Rhea went down, she had legally bound their fates together. There was no walking away.

"I hate your math, Mercer," Vance spat.

He pushed off the wall. His legs trembled so violently it looked like the stone beneath him was vibrating. He took a step forward, his boot catching on the edge of a cracked paver, and he nearly went down. He caught himself, his breath coming in ragged, shallow gasps.

He let the heavy Rebar slip from his right hand. It clanged against the floor, rolling away into the shadows.

[Memory Wipe Progress: 41%]

Vance reached the terminal. He didn't hesitate. He slammed both hands onto the top two cooling vents, his thick, calloused fingers curling around the brass louvers.

A sharp hiss of searing flesh filled the air.

Vance threw his head back and roared, a sound of pure, unadulterated agony. The veins in his neck bulged against his skin. His muscles, running on nothing but adrenaline and spite, locked into place.

[System Warning: Extreme Environmental Heat.]

"Hold it!" Rhea shouted, diving toward the gap Vance had just stabilized.

"Do it!" Vance screamed, his eyes squeezed shut, his teeth grinding together so hard Rhea thought they might shatter. Smoke was beginning to curl up from where his palms met the superheated brass. "Get the damn thing!"

[Memory Wipe Progress: 58%]

Rhea thrust her hands into the narrow opening between the vents. The heat inside the chassis was suffocating, a physical wall of pressure that felt like it was cooking her eyeballs. She squeezed her shoulders through, ignoring the burning scrape of the metal against her blazer.

The core of the terminal was a chaotic storm of blue light and spinning brass. In the center, suspended by beams of data, was the crystal. The raw data packet.

She reached for it.

[Warning: Administrative Firewall Detected.]
[Stamina Drain Active.]

The moment her fingers brushed the hard-light beams surrounding the crystal, a shockwave of cold, numbing energy blasted up her arms. It wasn't physical pain; it was a direct attack on her resource pool.

[Stamina: 10/100]

Rhea gasped, her lungs seizing. The System was trying to bankrupt her stamina to force her to let go. It was the same predatory mechanic that governed gathering wood or picking fungus, scaled up to protect the Overseer's secrets.

"I am... slipping!" Vance choked out, his knees buckling. The vents shifted, closing another fraction of an inch, pressing against Rhea's ribs.

"Unit 734!" Rhea yelled, her voice strained. "Divert the firewall! Give me three seconds!"

[Unit 734 Efficiency: 32%]

The drone zipped forward, hovering just outside the vents. "Diverting administrative firewall will register as a direct cybernetic assault on a superior officer! The penalty is deletion!"

"If he dies," Rhea grunted, nodding toward Vance, "the contract defaults! You die anyway! Do the math, you rusted tin can!"

[Memory Wipe Progress: 79%]

The drone's optical lens flashed rapidly between red and green. The machine logic crashed against the localized legal paradox Rhea had built. The party contract demanded it protect its members to avoid its own destruction. The System demanded it protect the terminal.

The contract won.

Unit 734 extended a thin, copper interfacing needle from its chassis and jammed it directly into the terminal's external data port.

[System Alert: Unauthorized Override Attempt.]
[Firewall Diverted.]
[Unit 734 Efficiency: 25% -> 15%]

The blue hard-light beams surrounding the crystal flickered and dimmed. The numbing cold vanished from Rhea's arms.

[Stamina: 6/100]

She lunged forward, her fingers closing around the pulsing, jagged edges of the Raw Data Packet. It was heavy, far heavier than a piece of software had any right to be. It felt like holding a chunk of solid ice that was vibrating at a high frequency.

"Got it!" Rhea screamed, pulling her arms back.

But the crystal was tethered. A single, thick cable of glowing blue light connected the base of the raw data packet to the floor of the chassis.

[Memory Wipe Progress: 91%]

"It's anchored!" Rhea shouted, pulling with all her remaining strength. The tether stretched but didn't break. "Vance, I need more room! I have to rip it out!"

Vance didn't answer with words. He answered with a guttural, wet snarl. He shifted his footing, his boots sliding in his own blood, and pulled backward. The muscles in his back tore. The brass vents groaned in protest, bending slightly under the impossible strain of a man refusing to die.

The gap widened by two inches.

Rhea planted her boots against the lower chassis panel, wrapped both hands around the Raw Data Packet, and threw her entire body weight backward.

[Stamina: 2/100]

With a sound like shattering glass, the blue tether snapped.

Rhea flew backward, tumbling out of the terminal and hitting the stone floor hard. The breath was knocked from her lungs in a violent rush.

[Memory Wipe Progress: 99%]
[Memory Wipe Progress: 100%]
[Protocol Complete. Core Purged.]

The terminal's lights instantly died. The strobing red klaxons cut off, plunging the damp chamber into a sudden, ringing silence. The heavy brass vents slammed completely shut with a deafening clang, sealing the empty, burnt-out core.

Rhea holds the extracted raw data packet as the terminal finally crashes, securing the evidence. 

Vance released the scorching cooling vents with a curse, dropping to the stone floor as Rhea clutched the glowing data crystal.

Vance lay on his back, his chest heaving, his hands raw and blistered. His health bar was a terrifying sliver of crimson, hovering at 3/100, but it had stopped flashing. He had survived.

Unit 734 dropped from the air, its anti-gravity thrusters sputtering as it hit the dirt with a metallic thud. 

[Efficiency: 12%] 

Smoke curled from its interfacing needle.

Rhea sat up slowly, every muscle in her body screaming in protest. Her stamina was completely bottomed out, leaving her feeling hollow and weak. But in her lap, casting a soft, pulsing blue light over the grim scene, was the Raw Data Packet.

It was real. It was physical. And it was exactly the kind of leverage that could break a world.

[Item Acquired: Raw Data Packet.]
[Quality: Administrative (Unique).]

Rhea let out a long, shaky breath, a grim smile touching the corners of her mouth. The Overseer wanted to play a game of ledgers and audits. Now, she had his books.

"Alright," Rhea whispered, her voice hoarse but steady. "Let's go file a complaint."
