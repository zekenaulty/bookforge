With his golden shields stripped and dissolving into digital dust, the overseer howled as his bureaucratic form twisted, mutating into a feral boss monster of jagged code and bulging grey muscle. Across the room, the glowing purple chains shattered, and Vance's Debt Lock broke.

[Status Effect Removed: Debt Lock]

Freed from the system's paralysis, Vance collapsed to one knee, gasping for air. His prominently blistered hands tightened around the grip of his Rebar. He didn't have the health for a prolonged fight.

[Vance HP: 2/100]
[Rhea Stamina: 0/150]
[Boss Encounter: Feral Overseer. HP: 15,000/15,000]

The mutated overseer roared, a sound like tearing metal, and lunged across the polished stone floor. Rhea, her lungs burning and the noticeably singed hair on her arms standing on end from the static charge of the room, scrambled backward. She shoved the Raw Data Packet and the Null-Value Token into her left blazer pocket, freeing her hands. She couldn't dodge. She had no stamina left to outrun a feral system administrator.

"The desk!" Rhea screamed, throwing her shoulder against a shattered mahogany pillar from the ruined double doors. 

Unit 734 hovered erratically near the wall, its smoking chassis venting thick plumes of warning exhaust. The drone was useless in combat, but it provided a momentary distraction. The feral overseer swiped at the drone, missing it but embedding his massive, grey-muscled fist into the reinforced wall of the inner office. 

Vance didn't hesitate. Leveraging his Heavy Blunt-Force Trauma, he used the momentum of his ruined body to swing the Rebar directly into the back of the overseer's knee. The metal clanged against the corrupted code-flesh.

[Rebar Durability: 58%]
[Critical Hit! Feral Overseer HP: 14,800/15,000]

It wasn't enough damage, but it forced the monster off-balance. The overseer stumbled forward, crashing into the side of his own massive obsidian desk. 

"Push!" Vance bellowed. 

Rhea jammed the splintered mahogany pillar under the lip of the heavy obsidian desk, using it as a lever. Vance threw his entire weight against the opposite side. The office architecture groaned. The feral overseer tried to right himself, his jagged claws scraping across the polished stone, but the combined leverage of the makeshift fulcrum and Vance's desperate shove tipped the scales. 

The massive obsidian desk, weighing several tons of administrative malice, flipped over. It slammed down onto the mutated overseer, pinning him against the unyielding stone floor. The sickening crunch of code and bone echoed through the inner office.

[Environmental Damage: 14,800]
[Boss HP: 0/15,000]
[Kill Confirmed: Feral Overseer]

Vance brought his Rebar down on the mutated overseer's exposed skull just to be sure, shattering the entity into glowing white fragments. The overseer's HP hit zero, securing the kill for the party.
