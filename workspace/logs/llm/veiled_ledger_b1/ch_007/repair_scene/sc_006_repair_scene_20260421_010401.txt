With his golden administrative shields stripped and dissolving into a cascade of useless digital dust, the overseer let out a sound that defied the sterile acoustics of the inner office. It was a howl of pure, unadulterated system error—a screech of tearing metal and failing logic gates. His pristine bureaucratic form twisted violently. The tailored suit he wore ripped at the seams as bulging, necrotic grey muscle expanded underneath. Jagged lines of corrupted code pulsed beneath his newly formed, feral skin.

Across the room, the glowing purple chains of administrative paralysis shattered into a million harmless polygons. 

[Status Effect Removed: Debt Lock]

Freed from the system's absolute hold, Vance Harrow collapsed to one knee, his chest heaving as his lungs desperately pulled in the stale, ozone-tinged air of the sanctum. His prominently blistered hands tightened around the cold, unforgiving grip of his Rebar. Every nerve in his body screamed in protest. The Heavy Blunt-Force Trauma status effect throbbed in his temples like a secondary heartbeat. He didn't have the health for a prolonged fight. He barely had the health to stand.

[Boss Encounter Initiated: Feral Overseer]
[Feral Overseer HP: 15000/15000]

The mutated overseer roared again, a sound that rattled the polished stone floor and sent a fresh wave of static electricity through the room. Rhea Mercer scrambled backward, her boots slipping on the slick marble. Her lungs burned with the absolute zero of her stamina bar. The noticeably singed hair on her bare forearms stood on end from the ambient static charge radiating off the boss monster. 

She looked down at her hands. The Raw Data Packet pulsed in her right hand, heavy with the weight of the system's stolen ledger. The Null-Value Token sat cold and heavy in her left. Neither was a weapon. Neither was going to stop a fifteen-thousand hit-point monstrosity currently eyeing her like a localized deletion protocol.

"Move!" Vance rasped, his voice barely a croak.

Rhea didn't need to be told twice. She shoved the Raw Data Packet and the Null-Value Token into her left blazer pocket, freeing her hands. She couldn't dodge with finesse. She had no stamina left to outrun a feral system administrator. She had to rely on raw, ugly momentum. 

"The desk!" Rhea screamed, throwing her shoulder against a shattered mahogany pillar that had once framed the ruined double doors. 

The feral overseer lunged. He didn't run; he propelled himself forward with terrifying, glitching bursts of speed, his massive grey-muscled arms tearing up chunks of the polished stone floor with every step. 

Unit 734 hovered erratically near the wall, its smoking chassis venting thick plumes of warning exhaust that smelled of melting copper and ozone. The drone was completely useless in direct combat, its chassis integrity hovering at a miserable thirty-four percent, but its erratic flight path provided a momentary, desperate distraction. The feral overseer swiped at the drone with a hand the size of a manhole cover, missing the metallic chassis by inches but embedding his massive fist deep into the reinforced wall of the inner office. Plaster and digital wireframes exploded outward.

Vance didn't hesitate. Leveraging the agonizing pain of his Heavy Blunt-Force Trauma, he used the momentum of his ruined body to launch himself forward. He wasn't aiming for the head or the heavily muscled torso. He aimed for the structural weak point of any bipedal entity, digital or otherwise. He swung the Rebar with everything he had left, driving the rusted iron directly into the back of the overseer's knee. 

The metal clanged against the corrupted code-flesh with a sickening thud. 

[Rebar Durability: 58%]
[Critical Hit! Feral Overseer HP: 14800/15000]

It wasn't nearly enough damage to kill the thing. Two hundred points of damage against a fifteen-thousand point health pool was a rounding error. But it wasn't about the damage. It was about the physics engine. 

The strike forced the massive monster off-balance. The overseer stumbled forward, his leg buckling just enough to send his center of gravity pitching wildly out of control. He crashed hard into the side of his own massive obsidian desk, the impact shaking the entire room. 

"Push!" Vance bellowed, spitting blood onto the marble. 

Rhea was already moving. She jammed the splintered mahogany pillar under the lip of the heavy obsidian desk, wedging it against the stone floor to use as a makeshift lever. Her muscles screamed, her zero-stamina state threatening to plunge her into unconsciousness, but she threw her entire body weight onto the end of the pillar. 

Vance threw his shoulder against the opposite side of the desk, his blistered hands finding purchase on the smooth, dark stone. The office architecture groaned under the immense pressure. The feral overseer snarled, trying to right himself. His jagged claws scraped frantically across the polished stone, leaving deep, glowing gouges in the floor as he tried to find leverage to push himself back up. 

But the combined leverage of Rhea's makeshift fulcrum and Vance's desperate, adrenaline-fueled shove tipped the scales. The massive obsidian desk—weighing several tons of pure administrative malice and dense digital rendering—passed its tipping point. 

It flipped over with a sound like a collapsing building. 

It slammed down directly onto the mutated overseer, pinning the feral monstrosity against the unyielding stone floor. The sickening crunch of corrupted code and simulated bone echoed through the inner office, followed immediately by a massive, cascading fountain of red damage numbers.

[Environmental Damage: 14800]
[Feral Overseer HP: 0/15000]

The system couldn't calculate the sheer crushing weight of its own unyielding architecture against a pinned entity. The environmental damage maxed out the boss's remaining health pool in a single, catastrophic frame. 

The overseer let out one final, gurgling screech of static before his form began to violently destabilize. 

Vance, breathing heavily, stood over the edge of the overturned desk. He raised his Rebar high, his blistered hands trembling with exhaustion, and brought the iron down on the mutated overseer's exposed, glitching skull just to be absolutely sure. 

The entity shattered into a million glowing white fragments, dissolving into the cracking stone floor. 

[Kill Confirmed: Feral Overseer]

Rhea slumped against the overturned desk, her chest heaving as the glowing white fragments faded into nothingness. The room began to violently shake, the local node losing its anchor to reality. They had done it. They had actually killed the house.
