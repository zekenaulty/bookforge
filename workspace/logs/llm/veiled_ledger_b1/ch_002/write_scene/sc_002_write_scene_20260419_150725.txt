PROSE:
Rhea leaned heavily against the petrified bark, her lungs burning as she sucked in the cold, drafty air. She was completely winded. In her left hand, she gripped the single Ash-Fungus she had just pulled from the tree. It pulsed with a faint, mocking bioluminescence. 

Eighty-five stamina. The System had charged her an eighty-five stamina Gathering Tax just to pick a damn mushroom. 

[Stamina: 15/100]

The physical backlash of the tax felt like she had just sprinted up six flights of stairs while breathing through a wet towel. Her bruised left leg throbbed in time with her racing heart. She adjusted her grip on the Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon in her right hand, using its heavy weight as a crude walking stick to keep herself upright. 

The temperature in the Ash-Choked Forest was dropping. If she was going to survive the night, she needed more than a glowing mushroom. She needed basic firewood. 

A few yards away, a scatter of loose, gray branches lay half-buried in the powdery ground. They weren't attached to anything. They were just debris. Rhea dragged her boots through the ash, reaching down with two aching fingers to pinch the end of a small, dead twig. 

[Gathering Tax Assessed: Minor Debris. Stamina -5.]
[Stamina: 10/100]

A fresh wave of exhaustion hit her knees like a physical blow. She stumbled, barely catching herself with the bludgeon. 

"You have got to be kidding me," Rhea rasped, staring at the twig. "It was on the ground. It’s literal garbage."

The System did not care. The System was a ledger, and the ledger demanded its cut. 

Desperate, she reached for a slightly thicker branch, hoping the yield would justify the cost. Her fingers brushed the coarse wood.

[Gathering Tax Assessed: Moderate Debris. Stamina -10.]
[Stamina: 0/100]
[Status Effect: Exhaustion applied.]

Her vision swam. The edges of the pale forest blurred into a sickening, bruised purple. The interface flickered wildly in the center of her vision, expanding from a standard notification into a blaring, crimson alert.

[Insufficient Stamina for Transaction.]
[Stamina Deficit will be drawn from HP pool at a 1:1 ratio.]
[Warning: Current HP is 1/100. Deficit deduction will result in fatal Indenture Default.]

Rhea froze. The math was perfectly, horribly clear. If she picked up the stick, the System would charge her life force. With exactly one hit point to her name, the cost of picking up a piece of firewood was death. The rigged economy wasn't just designed to keep her in debt; it was designed to bankrupt her out of existence before nightfall.

Slowly, agonizingly, she let go of the branch. She let her arm drop to her side. 

Rhea slumped against a jagged boulder by the dry riverbed and slid down until she hit the ash. She sat perfectly motionless, clutching her single mushroom, refusing to interact with the environment, refusing to trigger the fatal taxes that governed every inch of this miserable world.
