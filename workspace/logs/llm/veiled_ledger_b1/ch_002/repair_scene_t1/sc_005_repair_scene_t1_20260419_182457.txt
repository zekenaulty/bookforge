PROSE:
With the scavenger crushed and untaxed loot secured, Rhea turned her attention to the raw materials. The creature’s brittle carapace had shattered under the heavy deadfall, leaving behind splintered wood and several glowing, fruit-like nodes of fungus.

She slumped against a nearby processing rock in the Ash-Choked Forest, the physical toll of her situation screaming in every nerve. Her peripheral vision was dominated by a pulsing, obnoxious red border.

[HP: 1/100]

[Stamina: 0/100]

[Status Effect: Exhaustion (Active)]

Every breath she drew felt like inhaling powdered glass. She eyed the freshly dropped fungus from the dead scavenger, noting it was identical to the raw Ash-Fungus she already had safely stowed in her left blazer pocket. She reached out and pulled one of the scavenger's glowing nodes closer to the flat surface of the rock.

As she stared at the glowing node, a helpful blue box chimed into existence.

[Item Detected: Raw Ash-Fungus]

[Would you like to auto-refine this item into Minor Stamina Paste?]

[Refinement Fee: 5 Stamina. Notice: Insufficient Stamina detected. Overdraft protection active. Refinement Fee will be deducted from HP at a 200% penalty rate (10 HP).]

Rhea let out a dry, rattling laugh. The System wanted ten hit points to mash a mushroom. She only had one. Clicking 'Accept' would be instant suicide.

"Not today, you parasitic spreadsheet," she whispered.

She dismissed the prompt with a weak swipe of her hand. The blue box vanished. If the System treated environmental collisions as untaxed events, then manual labor done entirely outside the UI's automated shortcuts should theoretically bypass the refinement fee entirely. 

Rhea adjusted her grip on the Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon in her right hand. The weapon was crude and heavy, and lifting it took monumental effort. She lined up the heavy, flat end of the bludgeon over the scavenger's dropped fungus and let gravity do the work.

The heavy masonry slammed down onto the rubbery node, bursting it into a puddle of glowing, pale-blue paste. Spore-dust puffed into the air.

She waited for the inevitable blue box demanding a tax for unauthorized crafting. Nothing happened. The red border of her vision didn't flash. Her single hit point remained intact.

Keeping a firm grip on the Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon, she scooped up a handful of the glowing paste with her free hand and shoved the mashed fungus into her mouth. It tasted like battery acid mixed with chalk, but the System's response was glorious.

[Manual Processing Detected. Refinement fee bypassed.]

[Consumable Ingested: Mashed Ash-Fungus (Untaxed)]

[Stamina +40]

[HP +24]

[Status Effect: Exhaustion removed.]

A rush of cool energy flooded her veins. The heavy weight of the Exhaustion debuff evaporated as its icon shattered in her peripheral vision.

[HP: 25/100]

[Stamina: 40/100]

She let out a long exhale, wiping a smear of glowing spore-paste from her chin. She had beaten the rigged math twice in one day. Rhea's stats recover, leaving her ready to bank her surplus.
