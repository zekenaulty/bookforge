PROSE:
Rhea leaves the obsidian debris behind and climbs the steep, ash-choked slope toward a glowing stone pedestal that juts from the crater's ridge. 

Every step is an exercise in measured agony. Her bruised left leg throbs in time with her heartbeat, a dull ache radiating up her thigh. But the real problem isn't the leg. It's the math.

[HP: 1/100]

Walking around with exactly one percent of your life force feels like balancing a china plate on a broomstick in a hurricane. One bad trip, one sharp rock, one enthusiastic gust of sulfur-scented wind, and it's game over. The only thing keeping her corporeal is the persistent, blinking buff in the periphery of her vision.

[Status: Tutorial Grace Period (Active)]

A weird, numbing static hums over her pale skin, artificially capping the pain. It is a mechanical mercy, and Rhea inherently distrusts it. 

She reaches the top of the ridge, chest heaving. The right pocket of her ruined blazer sags with the dead weight of her Cracked Smartphone. She taps the fabric once, feeling the shattered glass through the wool—a useless brick of lithium and memories, but the only piece of Earth she has left. 

The wasteland stretches out before her from the vantage point of the ridge. It is an endless expanse of gray dunes and jagged volcanic rock, utterly devoid of life. The sky above is a bruised, swirling purple, bleeding light like a crushed neon tube. 

Except for the pedestal.

It sits ten yards away, perfectly square, carved from a smooth, pale stone that clashes violently with the surrounding geology. A soft blue light emanates from a holographic parchment hovering just above its surface. 

As Rhea limps closer, the System chimes—a bright, cheerful, bureaucratic ping that sets her teeth on edge. 

[System Prompt: Starter Cache Located!]
[Welcome to the Ledger's Frontier Tutorial Zone, Indentured Soul!]
[Would you like to claim your Basic Survival Provisions?]
[Includes: 1x Rations (Meager), 1x Water Skin (Stale), 1x Flint Dagger (Poor).]

Rhea stares at the floating blue text. Her throat is lined with sandpaper. Her stomach performs a hollow, desperate gymnastics routine. The offer is a lifeline. A canteen and a knife are the difference between bleeding out in an ash dune and actually making it to tomorrow. 

The prompt helpfully displays her ongoing existential nightmare in the margins, just to ensure she understands her leverage in this negotiation.

[Current Soul Debt: 100,000]

Right. The debt. The invisible chains the System slapped on her the moment she fell out of the sky. She doesn't know the exchange rate, but the title 'Indentured Soul' heavily implies she isn't in the wealth-building phase of this new reality. 

She steps up to the pedestal and raises her right arm. The System Indenture Brand on her left wrist itches with phantom heat as the holographic parchment waits, the word 'Accept' pulsing in an inviting, friendly font at the bottom of the screen. Her fingers tremble. She just needs a drink of water. 

Rhea stops her hand from hitting Accept, her eyes locking onto the tiny asterisk tucked into the corner of the holographic parchment.

It is barely a pixel wide. A microscopic star of legal doom, perfectly camouflaged against the glowing blue border. The math was already bad, but suddenly, the math looks exactly like a trap.
