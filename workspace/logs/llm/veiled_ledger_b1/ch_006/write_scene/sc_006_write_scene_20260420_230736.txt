PROSE:
After navigating the treacherous gray dunes of the Ash Wastes, the party reached the jagged outskirts of the administrative hub and immediately took cover behind a massive basalt boulder.

Vance slumped against the stone, his breath rattling in his chest as he gripped his Rebar with heavily blistered hands. His health bar was a razor-thin red line, still frozen at exactly 10 HP. Escaping the tollbooth had been a miracle of bad programming—Rhea’s desperate physics exploit of jamming Unit 734 into the doorframe had completely broken the hounds' pathing AI, letting them slip past the glitched enemies.

Rhea slid down the boulder next to the mechanical construct, its smoking chassis venting thin plumes of gray exhaust into the air. She rubbed her bare forearms, the singed arm hair still smelling faintly of ozone. Her own Stamina was locked at a miserable 1, the Exhaustion (Severe) status effect making every blink feel like lifting weights. But she couldn't stop. They were at the threshold.

She opened her interface.

[System Inventory Accessed.]

Reaching into the digital space, she withdrew the Heavy Iron Ingot. It hit the ash-covered ground with a dull, heavy thud. Next, she pulled out the Crystallized Experience—a jagged shard of pure, refunded value that pulsed with a violent purple light. These were the high-tier materials she needed.

She placed the glowing shard directly on top of the dense iron block.

[Crafting Interface Initialized.]

[Warning: Incompatible Materials.]

She didn’t need them to be compatible. She needed them to break the math. Using the administrative weight of her Audit Compliance (Red-Tier) debuff, she forced a manual synthesis, grinding the pure numerical value of the experience into the physical density of the iron to create a logical paradox.

[Override Authorized: Audit Compliance Active.]

[Consuming: Heavy Iron Ingot (x1), Crystallized Experience (x1)]

The purple light flared, violently compressing the iron until both items collapsed into a single point of absolute darkness. When the light faded, a heavy, light-swallowing coin rested in the ash.

[Item Created: Null-Value Token.]

Rhea scooped the cold coin up with her left hand.

She had successfully crafted the Null-Value Token. With her high-tier materials consumed in the paradox, Vance pushed himself off the rock, and the party prepared for the final breach.
