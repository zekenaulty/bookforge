Artie and Fizz evaded the shouting guards, slipping through a rusted iron grate and descending into the damp gloom of the Oakhaven Crypts to test his new gear. The heavy iron grate slammed shut behind them, the metallic clang echoing down the stone tunnel and abruptly cutting off the enraged shouts of the Adventurer's Guild.

Artie leaned against the cold, moss-slicked wall, his chest heaving. His pale, skinny-fat frame was completely unsuited for fleeing through medieval alleyways, let alone exploring subterranean dungeons. He brushed his messy brown hair out of his brown eyes.

[Status Effect Removed: Safe Zone Active]

The floating blue text hovered in Artie’s peripheral vision, a stark, terrifying reminder of his fragile mortality. He shivered, pulling the Cloak of the Cowardly Lion tighter around his shoulders. The garment was absurdly oversized, swallowing him entirely. He adjusted his grip on the Rusty Iron Dagger in his right hand, the metal cold and uncomfortably heavy.

"Are you absolutely certain this is a good idea?" Artie asked, his voice echoing slightly against the damp stone walls. "Because 'Safe Zone Active' just went away, and I am currently sitting at a grand total of one hit point."

[HP: 1/1]
[Stamina: 10/10]

Fizz floated lazily beside him, her neon-pink hair glowing like a tiny, pixelated beacon in the oppressive dark. Her digital-blue eyes sparkled against her translucent skin. "Relax, Artie! It’s a classic RPG exploit. High-level dungeon, high-tier loot, zero risk if you just don't get hit. The cloak provides a stealth modifier. You'll be practically invisible!"

Artie glanced down at himself. The Employee ID Badge still dangled from his neck lanyard, and the cursed Participation Trophy pinned to his lapel felt heavier than usual. "Practically invisible. Right."

He crept deeper into the tunnel. As they rounded a jagged corner, the narrow passage opened into a wide, ruined antechamber. In the center of the room stood a towering skeletal guard clad in rusted chainmail.

[Target: Skeletal Guard (Lvl. 8)]

"Okay," Artie whispered, his heart hammering against his ribs. "It's right there. What now?"

"Stealth time!" Fizz whispered back. "Keep to the shadows. The cloak's enchantment will mask your presence."

Artie took a cautious step forward. A soft, blue notification chimed in his vision.

[Stealth: Active (Flawed)]

Artie paused, his foot hovering an inch above the ground. "Flawed? Fizz, what does 'Flawed' mean?"

"Oh, it's just a minor modifier," Fizz said breezily. "It means the item's stealth is highly effective, but it has a... thematic drawback."

He didn't have to wait for an answer. As he shifted his weight to take his next step, the Cloak of the Cowardly Lion let out a loud, pathetic whimper. It sounded exactly like a frightened dog realizing it was at the vet.

The skeletal guard’s skull snapped up.

Artie panicked, trying to take a hasty step backward, but his heel came down hard on a brittle femur hidden beneath the dust. 

CRACK.

The cloak let out another loud, cowardly yelp, trembling violently against his back.

[Stealth Check: Failed]
[Aggro Gained: Skeletal Guard]

The heavy lion hood of the cloak slipped back, offering no concealment. Artie stood exposed as the skeleton turned its glowing eyes toward him.
