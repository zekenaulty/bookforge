The blade bounced off the creature’s bark-like hide with a dull, vibrating *thwack* that rattled all the way up to the shoulder socket. 

`[-2 Durability. Iron Shortsword is at 14/50.]`

Perfect. Just what was needed. At this rate, the sword would shatter before the oversized root vegetable even noticed it was being attacked. 

`[Target: Rabid Mandrake (Lvl. 3) – HP: 42/50]`

"Stop screaming," the adventurer muttered, shifting their grip to wipe sweat on a mud-caked tunic. "You're a plant. You don't even have lungs."

The mandrake did not care. It lunged, teeth—why did a plant have teeth?—snapping at knee height. 

A quick sidestep, a pivot, and a downward thrust putting the entire weight of the body behind the hilt. The blade caught the thing right where a central nervous system theoretically should have been. 

`[Critical Hit! -38 HP]`
`[Target Defeated.]`

The screech cut off instantly, replaced by the wet, heavy thud of the corpse hitting the dirt. The silence that followed was beautiful, ruined only by the smell of ozone and rotting compost. 

A familiar, crystalline chime rang out in the quiet. The dopamine hit was immediate, a warm rush of artificial satisfaction injected straight into the brainstem.

`[Experience Gained: 15 XP]`
`[Level Up! You are now Level 4.]`
`[+5 Unallocated Stat Points]`
`[System Notification: Skill Unlocked – Botanical Brutality (Passive) Lvl 1. You deal 5% more damage to aggressive flora. Because apparently, weeding is your true calling.]`

The adventurer stared at the blue, floating text. Five percent. It was a statistical insult. But it was also progress. They dismissed the window, opened their stat sheet, and dumped all five points straight into Strength. Survival in this hellscape wasn't about finesse or careful optimization. It was about hitting stupid things with heavy metal until they stopped moving, and then looting their corpses for pocket change. 

`[Strength is now 18.]`

The muscles in their arms seized, then flooded with a sudden, unnatural heat. The ache from the recoil vanished. They pulled the sword free from the dirt with a single, effortless jerk. Time to find another weed.
