With her immediate survival secured, Rhea bundled the remaining untaxed materials into the crook of her left arm and located a nearby automated System deposit node. The machine stood at the edge of the dry riverbed, half-buried in a drift of pale gray ash. It looked less like a mystical artifact and more like a rusted ATM that had been violently fused with an industrial trash compactor.

Her left leg throbbed with every step, the deep bruising a constant, dull ache that served as a reminder of her initial fall. But her breathing had finally leveled out. Her Stamina was holding steady at forty. She kept her Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon gripped tightly in her right hand, her knuckles white against the crude, heavy stone handle. The petrified forest around her was dead and quiet, save for the howling sulfur wind, but she wasn't about to trust the silence. The System had already proven it wanted her dead—or worse, endlessly working.

She limped the last few yards to the node. As she stepped within arm's reach, a rectangular slot on the obelisk's rusted face slid open with a screech of un-oiled metal, revealing a glowing blue hopper. The smell of ozone and burning copper wafted from the chute. Without hesitation, she dumped the splintered petrified wood and the cracked scavenger carapace into the grinding maw.

Instantly, a holographic screen snapped into existence, projecting a crisp, bureaucratic blue light into the gloom.

[Deposit Interface: Active]
[Contents Detected: Unregistered Biomass / Petrified Wood]
[Default Classification: Harvested Resources]
[Processing Tax: 5 Stamina]
[Yield: 4 Copper Credits]
[Notice: 100% of Yield auto-garnished toward Soul Debt]
[Accept? Y/N]

Rhea stared at the floating text, her jaw tightening. A bitter laugh scraped its way out of her throat. Five Stamina just to hand over the trash she’d tricked the environment into dropping. At her current capacity, that was over ten percent of her available physical energy. And the payout wouldn't even reach her pocket—it would vanish straight into the gaping, bottomless maw of her hundred-thousand-point deficit.

"Absolutely not," she muttered, jabbing her dirt-caked finger against the glowing red 'N'.

The machine let out a harsh, digitized buzz of disapproval. The hopper locked shut with a heavy metallic clunk, holding her items hostage.

Rhea leaned closer, her dark eyes scanning the glowing blue parchment. The System was a rigid bureaucracy. That was the one truth she had cemented since waking up in this ash-covered hellhole. Bureaucracies didn't cheat; they just hid the rules behind fine print, default settings, and artificial friction. They relied on exhaustion. They banked on the fact that a bleeding, starving arrival would just hit 'Accept' to make the prompt go away.

She ignored the flashing 'Accept' prompt that reappeared and tapped the text that read 'Default Classification'. Nothing happened. She swiped at it. Still nothing. Finally, tracing the edge of the interface frame, she spotted a microscopic, translucent gear icon tucked into the bottom right corner of the [Deposit Interface].

She pressed it.

The window expanded with a soft chime, unrolling a dense, scrolling list of alternative tax brackets and deposit categories. Most of them were worse. 'Hunted Spoils' carried a ten-point Stamina tax. 'Mined Ore' demanded fifteen. 'Salvaged Artifacts' required a flat HP deduction. The list went on, a litany of extortion designed to keep the indentured forever in the red.

She kept scrolling, her eyes tracking the tiny text as her contract_analysis skill hummed at the back of her mind, highlighting the subtle shifts in legal phrasing. Right at the very bottom, buried beneath a dozen obscure sub-clauses, was a category labeled 'Environmental Debris Clearance'.

She tapped it. The screen flickered, recalibrating the math.

[Transaction Log: Reclassified as 'Environmental Debris Clearance'.]
[Warning: This classification is reserved for civic maintenance. No gathering XP will be awarded.]
[Processing Tax: 0 Stamina]
[Yield: 4 Copper Credits]
[Notice: Civic maintenance stipends are exempt from standard Soul Debt garnishment.]
[Accept? Y/N]

Rhea grinned, a sharp, genuine expression that reached her dark brown eyes. She slammed her palm against the 'Y'.

The machine shuddered. Inside the obelisk, heavy gears ground the scavenger carapace and petrified wood into dust with a violent, satisfying crunch. A plume of pale exhaust vented from the top of the node. A moment later, a small drawer popped open at the base.

Inside sat four dull, rectangular metal coins. They didn't glow, and they weren't magical, but to Rhea, they were the most beautiful things she had seen all day.

She scooped them up, feeling their cold, heavy reality against her skin. A final notification chimed in her peripheral vision.

[Soul Debt: 100,000]
[Current Balance: 4 Copper Credits]

The debt was still a crushing, impossible number. But for the first time, she had capital that the System couldn't touch. She slipped the Copper Credits into her right blazer pocket, letting them clink softly against her stowed Cracked Smartphone.

Adjusting her grip on her Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon, Rhea successfully banked her first copper credits without incurring the standard gatherer's debt.
