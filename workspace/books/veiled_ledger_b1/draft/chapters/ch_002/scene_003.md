Sitting motionless against a jagged boulder to avoid fatal stamina drain, Rhea watched an ash-crusted scavenger mob interact with the petrified environment. 

She didn't dare blink too hard. Blinking felt like it might cost a fraction of a stamina point, and she had exactly zero left to spend.

[Stamina: 0/100]
[Status: Exhaustion. Warning: Further physical exertion will incur a direct HP penalty.]
[HP: 1/100]

One hit point. It wasn't a metaphor. At one hit point, her pulse didn't thud; it vibrated, a thin, frantic buzzing warning her that her structural integrity was failing. Her bruised left leg throbbed with a dull ache. 

In her left hand, her fingers loosely cradled the glowing Ash-Fungus. She had paid eighty-five stamina to pluck it, only for the System to offer a measly five stamina in return—assuming it didn't poison her. It was a payday loan payable in life force. In her right hand, her fingers rested limply against the rough grip of her Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon. If she tried to lift it right now, the exertion would overdraft into her health pool, and she would die.

So, she breathed the sulfur-tinged air in shallow sips and watched the local wildlife.

The scavenger, roughly the size of a badger but covered in a brittle crust of hardened ash, was currently engaged in a violent disagreement with a cluster of pale roots. It dug furiously at the base of a towering, petrified ash tree. 

Frustrated by a stubborn root, the creature reared back and slammed its front claws into the trunk. The impact echoed with a dull thud. High above, something cracked. A thick, gray branch snapped loose, plummeted downward, and slammed directly onto the scavenger’s armored back. The creature let out a startled yelp, scurried away, and resumed foraging in the dry riverbed.

The branch lay in the dirt. 

Rhea waited for the glowing blue box. When she had touched a branch earlier, the System had immediately flagged her with a Gathering Tax. But the scavenger had just knocked a branch loose, and there was no flashing red text. 

Her dark brown eyes narrowed. The System wasn't omnipresent magic; it was a digital ledger plastered on top of a physical reality. The scavenger wasn't a player, and it hadn't "gathered" the branch. It had hit a physical object, and gravity had done the rest. The System didn't tax kinetic energy. Once the branch was knocked loose by a physics collision, it was just loose geometry. 

A slow, sharp smile cracked the dried ash on Rhea’s face. She couldn't hit the trees herself without dying from exhaustion. But she didn't need to. She just needed the scavenger to hit the trees. 

She looked at the dry riverbed. The creature was digging near a steep embankment, directly below a precarious overhang of petrified roots and loose boulders. A natural deadfall trap. If she could startle it toward the overhang without officially attacking it, the physics engine would crush the mob. No combat tax. Clean, untaxed loot.

Gripping her heavy bludgeon just enough to use it as a walking cane, Rhea formulated a plan to exploit the physics engine, preparing to herd the mob.
