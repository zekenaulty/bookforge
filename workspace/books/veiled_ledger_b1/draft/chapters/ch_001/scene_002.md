Rhea gritted her teeth, her lungs fighting for air beneath the jagged obsidian debris that kept her pinned to the scorched earth. She couldn't even assess the damage to her trapped left leg, because a glowing blue notification hung exactly two inches from her nose, keeping her vision entirely blocked.

[Indenture Status: Pending Registration]
[Soul Debt Assessed: 100,000 Credits]
[Acknowledge terms to receive Standard Frontier Starter Pack and immediate Medical Relief.]
[Accept] | [Accept (Expedited)]

There was no 'Decline' button. Of course there wasn't.

A secondary window blinked into existence at the edge of her peripheral vision, pulsing with an angry red border.

[Warning: Environmental Hazard — Ashen Drop Zone]
[Passive Heat Damage: -1 HP / 15s]
[Time until initial damage tick: 12s...]

Rhea flicked her eyes to her own health bar, which hovered in the upper left corner of her vision like a sick joke.

[HP: 1/100]

Twelve seconds until the ambient temperature literally cooked the last hit point out of her body. 

"Come on," she wheezed, her voice cracking from the dry, sulfurous air. She tried to swipe the main notification away, but her ash-covered fingers passed right through the holographic projection. It was eye-tracking.

[Time until initial damage tick: 08s...]

She forced her eyes to focus on the microscopic text crawling along the very bottom edge of the blue box. The fine print. 

By clicking Accept, the Indentured Soul agrees to a 45 percent daily compounding interest rate on all outstanding debt, waives all rights to physical autonomy, and consents to biological liquidation in the event of default.

[Time until initial damage tick: 04s...]

"Not today, you bureaucratic nightmare," she muttered. She scanned the very bottom line. Nestled right next to the System version number and copyright date was a single, grayed-out word: Dismiss.

She focused her gaze on it, blinking hard to select it.

[Indenture Prompt Dismissed. Manual Onboarding selected.]
[Tutorial Grace Period protocol updated. Local environmental hazards suppressed.]
[Passive Heat Damage: Cancelled.]

The glowing blue text shattered into a shower of digital sparks, instantly clearing her field of view. 

The relief lasted a fraction of a second. Without the UI blocking her vision, Rhea could clearly see the massive, fractured rock pillar directly above her. A deep fissure snapped up its center, and the entire structure groaned, tilting downward. 

It was collapsing.

Adrenaline flooded her system. Rhea dug her hands into the hot ash and violently yanked her body backward. The Cracked Smartphone stowed in her right blazer pocket dug painfully into her hip, but she ignored it, twisting her torso and dragging her bruised left leg out from under the shifting rubble. 

She rolled across the smoking ground just as the obsidian pillar slammed into the exact spot where her head had been, throwing up a choking cloud of gray dust and razor-sharp shrapnel. 

Rhea lay flat on her back, coughing up ash as she stared at the empty, bruised sky. 

[Status Effect: Pinned removed.]

She was alive. Broke, battered, and stranded in a wasteland, but alive. And she hadn't signed a damn thing.
