[Item Durability: 28%]

[Action Denied: Asset flagged for administrative deletion.]

"Vance, Unit 734," Rhea snaps, her fingers flying across the glowing blue interface. "Acknowledge party contract override."

"What?" Vance coughs, wincing as he shifts his weight.

"Do it, or we die broke!" she barks, pulling up the scrolling green text of their custom agreement. 

[Party Contract: The Mercy Cache (Provisional) - Active]

Clause 4.B: Shared Debt and Liabilities. The system was rigged to punish debt, to bind it to the soul of the indentured so they could never escape it. Rhea was going to use that exact predatory logic.
