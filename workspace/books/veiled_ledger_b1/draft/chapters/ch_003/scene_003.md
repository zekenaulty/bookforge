[Party Quest Accepted: Vermin Control (Local)]
[Objective: Clear the collapsed transit tunnel.]

Rhea slipped the untaxed Copper Credits into her right blazer pocket, freeing up her left hand for balance as she hefted her Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon. The tunnel entrance loomed ahead, a jagged maw of shattered concrete and exposed rebar that smelled faintly of sulfur and wet rot. Inside, the clicking of ash-crusted scavengers echoed against the dark walls.

"Alright, muscle," Rhea whispered, glancing at Artie. The man looked like a walking corpse, his left arm hanging uselessly at his side and his chest a canvas of deep purple bruising. "You just need to get their attention. Do not get hit."

"I've got four hit points," Artie muttered, his right hand white-knuckling a jagged piece of rebar. "If a stiff breeze hits me, my soul gets repossessed."

[Party Telemetry: Artie HP 4/100. Status: Critical.]

"Then don't stand in the breeze," Vex supplied helpfully from the sidelines, his glowing blue holographic form hovering a safe distance from the tunnel entrance. "If you die, the paperwork for a sudden party dissolution is atrocious."

Artie grunted, stepping into the gloom of the collapsed transit tunnel. He banged his jagged piece of rebar against a rusted metal pipe, the screeching noise immediately drawing the red, glowing eyes of three scavengers. 

[Aggro Shift: Artie (Threat Level: High)]

The creatures hissed, their brittle carapaces scraping against the concrete as they lunged toward him. Artie held the scavengers' attention by a thread as Rhea crept along the shadows, raising her Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon as she prepared to drop the ceiling on them.
