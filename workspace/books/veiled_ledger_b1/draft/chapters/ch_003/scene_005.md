With the untaxed loot gathered—specifically the raw Scavenger Carapaces now shoved safely into her left blazer pocket alongside her obsidian shard and ash fungus—the group stood in the settling dust of the Collapsed Transit Tunnel to discuss formalizing their ad-hoc party into a binding System contract. 

Rhea flipped the sixteen Copper Credits in her left hand, the metal clinking with a satisfying, untaxed weight. "So," she said, eyeing the hovering drone chassis of Unit 734 and the battered, heavily scarred form of Vance Harrow. "You want to make this official. Pull up the paperwork."

Unit 734’s optical lens whirred, projecting a glowing blue interface into the damp air between them. 

[Party Contract: Default System Agreement]

Vance crossed his arms, his rebar resting against his shoulder. "Standard split. We share XP, we share drops. It’s how the frontier works."

Rhea tapped her fingers against her right blazer pocket, feeling the reassuring bulk of her Cracked Smartphone. She didn't need a working phone to spot a bad deal; she had spent ten years investigating corporate fraud. She leaned in, reading the scrolling green text beneath the main header. 

[Terms and Conditions: Standard Frontier Yield]

"Section four, subsection B," Rhea read aloud, her voice echoing off the fractured concrete. "In the event of accidental party casualty, surviving members inherit the deceased's unbanked assets minus a forty percent administrative fee. That’s a friendly-fire incentive if I’ve ever seen one."

"It's the default," Unit 734 buzzed, its synthetic voice flat. "The Ledger does not negotiate."

"The Ledger is a spreadsheet, and spreadsheets have cells you can overwrite," Rhea countered. She reached out and tapped the glowing projection, forcing the window to expand into raw, editable syntax. She began deleting lines of code, replacing the predatory default language with aggressively restrictive legal jargon. No friendly fire. No unilateral asset liquidation. No skimming the untaxed margins. 

[Liability Clause: Custom Override Initiated]

Vance blinked at the wall of text now glowing in the tunnel. "What is that?"

"That is a mutually assured destruction clause," Rhea said, her eyes reflecting the harsh light of the terminal. With a final, decisive swipe of her hand, Rhea finalized the custom contract syntax, presenting the ironclad liability agreement to Vance and Unit 734.
