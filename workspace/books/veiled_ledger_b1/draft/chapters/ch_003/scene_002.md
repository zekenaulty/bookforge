Rhea stood cornered at the rusted deposit node, her knuckles white around the handle of her Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon. She was caught entirely off guard, trapped between Vex's scrolling holographic threats of account deletion and Artie's desperate demands for the secret to her untaxed survival. She kept her left hand tightly clenched, hiding the four Copper Credits she had just banked, as the blue light of the terminal washed over the ash-choked clearing.

"You have incurred a Category Four UI Violation," Vex droned, his translucent azure silhouette flickering with bureaucratic indignation. A glaring red banner hovered just above his head.

[System Audit Flag: Pending Administrative Review]

"The standard penalty for exploiting physics collisions to bypass the gathering tax is an immediate administrative ban," the clerk continued, tapping a glowing clipboard that didn't actually exist. "I am required to quarantine your account and reset your physical form to the nearest sub-basement processing grinder."

"Don't you dare," Artie rasped. He spat a wad of bloody saliva onto the pale ash, clutching his broken left arm against his bruised purple chest. His wild eyes locked onto Rhea. "Tell me how you bypassed the stamina drain. Tell me right now. I'm dying here."

Rhea didn't move. She kept her weapon raised just enough to be a deterrent. A small, clinical window hovered near Artie's shoulder.

[Target: Artie]
[HP: 4/100]
[Status: Stage Two Systemic Shock, Minor Hemorrhage]

Rhea checked her own internal telemetry. She was sitting at twenty-five hit points and forty stamina. Better than Artie, but not nearly enough to survive a physical brawl with a desperate man, let alone whatever digital hell a system clerk could unleash. She couldn't fight them both. 

"An audit means a regional supervisor has to look at your specific node, Vex," Rhea said, keeping her voice perfectly level. "They'll see that you left a physics loophole open. How much paperwork is a Category Four violation going to generate for you?"

Vex's azure light pulsed rapidly. "The node's parameters are set by the Overseer. Do you have any idea how many forms I have to file when a soul gets ground up? It's seventeen pages. Front and back."

"And if the ledger balances, there's no anomaly," Rhea countered. She slowly lowered the Makeshift Masonry Bludgeon, letting its heavy end rest gently against the ash. "Artie needs untaxed resources to survive the hour. You need this audit flag to disappear without a trace. I have the exploit that can solve both problems."

Artie coughed, a wet, rattling sound. "So what's the play?"

Rhea reached out with her left hand, the cold metal of the Copper Credits pressing into her palm, and tapped the node's central crystal interface. "We form a party. A formal, System-recognized joint venture."

"A System party with a clerk?" Artie muttered. "He's a glowing blue hologram."

"It is a highly irregular gray area," Vex said, floating closer to the terminal. "If the party generates enough untaxed copper to cover the projected ledger deficit, the local system will classify the anomaly as a creative economic initiative. But if you fail, the Overseer will notice, and I will be formatted."

"Then we don't fail," Rhea said. She navigated the rusted terminal's glowing interface, pulling up the local group mechanics. With a sharp swipe, she projected a party interface window into the air.

[Party Formation Request]
[Leader: Rhea]
[Members: Vex (Clerk), Artie (Indentured Soul)]
[Terms: Shared Ledger Liability]

"Accept it," Rhea told Artie. "Or bleed out right here in the ash."

Artie reached out with his trembling right hand and slammed his palm against the glowing blue 'Accept' rune. Vex stared at the floating holographic parchment for a long moment, let out a sound like static interference, and tapped the prompt as well.

[Party Formation: Accepted]
[System Audit Flag: Suppressed]

The glaring red warning banner above Vex dissolved into a shower of harmless blue particles. The terminal chimed softly, registering the new systemic bond.
