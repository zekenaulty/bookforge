The party stood before a massive firewall of deletion code at the edge of the frontier zone, the roaring heat of the purge wave closing in behind them. The air tasted like ozone and burning copper. Rhea Mercer leaned heavily against a fractured obsidian pillar, her lungs burning as her stamina rested firmly at absolute zero. Behind them, the sanctum was already gone, replaced by a churning void of red hex-code that was eating the ground beneath their boots.

[System Notification: Deletion Wave Imminent. 10 seconds to sector scrub.]

Vance Harrow shifted his weight, his blistered hands tightening around his iron Rebar. Beside him, Unit 734 vented a thick plume from its smoking chassis, its optical sensor flashing a frantic, erratic yellow. 

"Any day now, Mercer," Vance grunted, eyeing the approaching red wall. 

[Vance Harrow HP: 45/100]

Rhea didn't have the breath to snap back. She raised her right hand, her fingers curled tightly around the glowing Administrative Residue Token. The token pulsed with a sickly, illicit light, humming against her palm. She staggered forward, dragging her leaden legs toward the boundary, and jammed the token directly into the firewall's glowing interface node. 

The system hesitated. For a terrifying second, the deletion wave surged forward, close enough to singe the hair on Rhea's arms. Then, the firewall shuddered.

[Access Granted. Administrative Override Accepted.]

The red code parted, tearing open a jagged, rectangular breach. Cold, sterile air rushed through the gap. 

"Go!" Rhea rasped. 

Vance didn't hesitate. He grabbed Unit 734 by its remaining functional handle and hurled the drone through the breach, diving in right after it. The firewall immediately began to close, the system fighting to correct the anomaly. 

Rhea threw herself forward. She tumbled through the shrinking gap just as the deletion wave crashed into the boundary behind her. The roar of the purge cut off instantly. Rhea hit the ground hard, her shoulder slamming into cold, paved stone. She lay there, gasping for air, staring up at the towering, impossible architecture of the Civic Layer Outskirts. They were out.
