Vance Harrow didn't have the luxury of dodging. His Heavy Blunt-Force Trauma status made every step look like he was dragging his boots through wet cement.

[Durability Check: Rebar -18%.]

"End of the hall!" Vance shouted, lowering his shoulder as they closed the distance.
