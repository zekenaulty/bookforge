Series Premise
- A dead fraud investigator is reborn into a world governed by a decaying System called the Ledger and survives by finding contractual exploits in rules everyone else treats as sacred.

Book Premise
- Rhea Mercer dies in a municipal records fire and wakes in a tutorial frontier where every starter system is built to skim value from new arrivals, forcing her to survive by auditing reality faster than reality can kill her.

Book Role
- Opening book of a trilogy.
- Establish the protagonist, the Ledger, the exploit loop, and the fact that local survival systems are part of a larger institutional extraction machine.

Hard Constraints
