# Series Brief: The Veiled Ledger

Series ID: `veiled-ledger`  
Author Ref: `eldrik-vale/v2`

## Premise
A dead fraud investigator is reborn into a frontier world governed by a decaying System called the Ledger and survives by finding contractual exploits in rules everyone else treats as sacred.

## Tone
Sharp, funny, tactically clear, mechanically exact, with accumulating political and metaphysical dread.

## Arc Goals
- Prove the System is political machinery, not neutral reality.
- Track the protagonist's growth from local exploiter to existential systems threat.
- Let progression gains create social, economic, and moral consequences.
- Escalate from frontier survival to civic corruption to world-engine failure.

## Series Promises
- Progression must feel earned through effort, risk, or rule exploitation.
- Humor comes from lived friction inside the mechanics, not detached mockery.
- Institutions must react to power in economic, social, legal, and religious ways.
- Reveals should recontextualize earlier material rather than replace it.

## Open Threads
- Who designed the Ledger and what is it really containing?
- Why do exploit paths exist and who profits from them?
- What is the true cost of keeping the System alive?
- Can the world survive reform if the System is foundational?

## Invariants
- No world resets.
- No convenient amnesia as a continuity device.
- Mechanical rules must stay consistent once established.
- Progression must create downstream social and political consequence.
- Humor cannot erase cost; it can only disguise or delay it.

## Protagonist
Rhea Mercer, mid-30s, former municipal fraud investigator / compliance analyst. Dry, procedural, skeptical, competent, and better at reading systems than trusting people.

## Book Shapes
- Book 1: frontier survival, tutorial exploitation, found-family start, local scam reveals systemic corruption.
- Book 2: city systems mystery, miracle accounting, guild/church/civic pressure, proof that the corruption is structural.
- Book 3: world-engine failure, faction war over the truth, reform vs collapse, trilogy payoff with consequence.
